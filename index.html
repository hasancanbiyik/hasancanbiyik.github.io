About
Background

I'm a computational linguist based in the US, with a Master's of Science degree in Computational Linguistics from Montclair State University, specializing in AI and machine learning. My research sits at the intersection of NLP and cross-lingual transfer, specifically how meaning shifts across languages in subtle, culturally-loaded ways.

My published work covers euphemism detection in Turkish and English, sarcasm detection in sitcom dialogue, and cross-lingual transfer learning using transformer models. I've presented at ACL-affiliated venues and managed annotation pipelines across multiple languages.

Outside research, I build things: production RAG systems, AWS-deployed models, and full-stack NLP tools from training scripts to web interfaces. I'm targeting AI, NLP, AI engineering, chatbot development, LLM fine-tuning roles while continuing to publish.
